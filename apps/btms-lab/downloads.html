Simulated BTMS dataset (57,648 rows, CSV in a zip)

Same shape as the experiment: 4 PCM options × 2 cooling modes × 6 cells × 1,201 seconds. Columns: pcm_code, pcm, cooling_code, cooling, time_s, cell, temperature_c, cooling_efficiency_eta. Generated by the lumped energy-balance model in btms/physics.py (seed 0) with 0.05 °C sensor noise. Simulated, not measured: use it to learn and test the pipeline.

PCM properties (paper, Table 2)

DSC first-deviation and onset temperatures, latent heat, densities, specific heats and thermal conductivities of OM42, OM48 and paraffin, as reported by Mane et al. (2026).

Experimental data sample (authors' repository)

The authors' public GitHub repository has a 100-row sample (one PCM, one cooling mode, one cell, 0–99 s) and their MATLAB code. The full 57,648-row dataset is needed for research results; it is available from the authors.

Full source code: btms-ml (Python)

Data loading, physics model, splits, features, the paper's five models, the PINN, conformal intervals, permutation importance, the design explorer, experiment runners, web export and 12 tests. Instructions in README.md.

Trained models and results (JSON)

model.json: weights of the three networks (PINN on all data, PINN without OM42, plain network without OM42), learned physical constants and the PCM table. results.json: experiment metrics and the design sweep. parity.json: reference outputs from PyTorch, used by the self-test.
