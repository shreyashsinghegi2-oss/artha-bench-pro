Research lab · Battery thermal management

Physics-informed ML for PCM + heat-pipe battery cooling

A neural network that learns cell temperature from data while obeying the energy balance heat in = heat stored + heat removed. It extends Mane et al., Journal of Energy Storage 180 (2026) 124313, with honest testing on unseen materials and a PCM design explorer.

4 · Experiment results

The paper's five models rebuilt in Python, then tested honestly. E1 uses the paper's random split; E2–E4 hold out one PCM at a time; E5 checks whether 90% uncertainty intervals really cover 90% of unseen-material cases. Errors are in °C.

90% conformal intervals on a held-out PCM

Physical constants the PINN learned from the data

Method. Lumped energy balance C_eff(T)·dT/dt = Q − G·(T − T_amb), with the PCM's latent heat as an apparent heat capacity over its melting range. Network: 9 inputs → 64 → 64 → 1, tanh. Loss = data error + energy-balance residual + initial condition. Full explanation on How it works; data and code on Data & code.

Status. Built by Shreyash Singh (Ajeenkya DY Patil University) on the experimental setup of Mane et al. (2026). Results here are a methods demonstration; they become research findings only after retraining on the experimental dataset.
