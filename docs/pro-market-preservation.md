# Non-breaking preservation boundaries

This upgrade is intentionally additive. It does not remove or rename existing routes, provider adapters, dashboard market sections, personal-finance modules, AI reliability features, learning tools, dark/light themes, disclosures or paper-trading storage.

The existing Finance Overview remains the entry dashboard. New Pro market workspaces are reached through compact entry cards and dedicated market navigation instead of replacing the Overview layout.

The existing India ticker, existing India Market Explorer, US Market Performance, provider-health diagnostics, FRED/World Bank economic data, business news, dashboard ArthaMind assistant, crypto workspace, personal-finance workspace and evaluation/reliability tools remain independent components.
