Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short: your financial data is yours. We use it only to give you answers and plans, protect it with encryption and access rules, never sell it, and let you export or delete it at any time.

This policy explains what personal data ArthaMind AI and the Artha Bench Pro workspace collect, why, and the controls you keep, in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. See also our Data protection page and Terms & conditions.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, country, and sign-in information.
  • Financial details you choose to enter: income, expenses, loans, investments, goals and documents you upload.
  • Questions you ask the AI CFO, and, if you turn it on, saved conversation history.
  • Basic usage data such as pages visited and errors, to keep the service working.

How we use it

  • To calculate your results, plans and reports.
  • To answer your questions in your chosen language.
  • To run your account, provide support and keep the service secure.

We do not sell your personal data or show you ads based on it.

Without an account

Calculators, markets and learning work without signing in. Data you enter as a guest stays in your own browser.

With an account

Data you save is stored with our database provider, encrypted in transit and at rest. Access rules make each record readable only by your signed-in account.

AI and your data

The AI receives the question you type. Sharing your saved records with the AI is off by default and can be switched on or off for each category: income and expenses, budgets and goals, portfolio, learning progress and conversation history.

Your rights and controls

  • Export your saved data.
  • Correct your details at any time.
  • Delete saved AI conversations or financial data.
  • Delete your whole account.
  • Withdraw consent for AI access to any category.

Security

Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and never stored by us in readable form. Service keys stay on our servers. We will never ask for your bank password, card PIN or OTP.

Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete data or your account, it is removed from our active systems, except where the law requires us to keep it.
